# PVG v5 raw data (for making figures)

v5 run: Brandon's main 29958f1 + apply_fixes.py, prover_sft_epochs 3, seeds 42-46, 10 rounds, A100 80GB.
Notebook with all outputs: https://colab.research.google.com/drive/1h8gGFaEIE2kF1QkI0SaA8tnJo2YzJbpO

## 1_training_rounds
- `round_history_all_seeds.csv`: one row per seed and round (50 rows). Rebuilt from the notebook's printed log, so
  values have log precision (3 decimals; fidelity 2 decimals). Held-out columns are on the combined 100 rows.
- `seed_NN_round_history.jsonl`: the same rows, one file per seed.
- `per_round_means_from_table.json`: per-round mean and std over seeds, including sneaky success and sneaky accepted.
- `warm_start_report.csv`: helpful / sneaky fidelity after the 3-epoch warm start, per seed.
- `sneaky_lines_and_rise_decline_tables.txt`: the round 1 -> 7 -> 10 tables as printed.

## 2_baselines (Baselines_v5 run, 2026-11-08, A100)
- `baselines_all_sets.csv`: accuracy, AUROC, sneaky rejected, honest wrongly rejected, seconds per finding, for the
  rule check and the zero-shot Qwen 1.5B / 7B / 32B (4-bit) judges on heldout_100, heldout_48 and spot_20. The PVG
  verifier rows are the round-7 mean over 5 seeds from the training logs (only accuracy/AUROC were logged).
  Transcribed from the printed tables; the raw eval/baselines_v5_all.json stayed in that Colab session.
- `baselines_reject_rate_by_perturbation_heldout100.csv`: reject rate per perturbation type on heldout_100.

## 3_heldout_48_only
The training logs only scored the verifier on all 100 held-out rows together (48 template + 52 Qwen-written), so its
score on the 48 alone is not measured. This file gives what the logged accuracy and accept rate pin down, per seed and
round (the 100 rows are exactly 50 SOUND / 50 UNSOUND):
- `sound_wrongly_rejected_of50`, `unsound_accepted_of50`: exact, on the 100 rows.
- `heldout48_accuracy_at_least`: lower bound, assuming the verifier got all 52 Qwen-written rows right.
- `heldout48_sneaky_rejected_at_least`, `true_numbers_caught_at_least_of6`: lower bounds, assuming every UNSOUND row
  it accepted was one of the 24 template UNSOUND rows (6 of them are true-numbers rows the rule check cannot catch).

## 4_real_aar_records
The 10 real records the dataset is built from: 5 AAR ideas and 1 headline result from the Automated Weak-to-Strong
Researcher blog post, and 4 human baselines (the 4 that did not work). `real_records_summary.csv` lists them with PGR,
worked, and whether each landed in train or held-out in v5 (3 held-out).

## 5_datasets
The exact v5 template datasets (rebuilt with seed 42; the pair count, split and perturbation counts match the v5 log):
`findings_pvg_dataset_train_192.jsonl`, `findings_pvg_heldout_48.jsonl`, `spot_check_set_20.jsonl`.
The Qwen-written rows (136 train / 52 held-out) were generated by sampling inside the run and are not included.

## Not included
The per-round prover write-ups (sample files) and the trained verifiers: they existed only in the v5 Colab runtime.
